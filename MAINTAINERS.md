# Maintainers and project decisions

**Primary maintainer: [Filip Volf (@Reckless98)](https://github.com/Reckless98).**
The repository owner controls scope, review, merges and releases. This is currently
a single-maintainer project; no external governance board or independent approval
process is implied.

Contributors propose changes through issues and pull requests. Explain the problem,
evidence, tradeoffs and scientific implications. Architectural, licensing and
release decisions must be explicit in a reviewable proposal. Routine maintenance
should preserve behavior and the provenance of existing results.

The maintainer may request revisions or decline changes that lack rights, evidence,
maintainability or a clear relationship to the research objectives. Disagreements
should stay focused on those criteria and follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

No change to the project license or publication of a release is authorized by the
modernization documents themselves. The rights review and maintainer approval must
be complete first. Merge permission is not the same as third-party copyright ownership.

Additional maintainers may be appointed by an explicit repository decision that
records their responsibilities. Keep this file and any code-ownership settings in
sync with actual access. No automatic co-maintainer or community-adoption claim is
made from commit author labels.

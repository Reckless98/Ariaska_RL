# Community code of conduct

Ariaska_RL welcomes participation across backgrounds and experience levels.
Treat people respectfully, critique evidence and code, and explain disagreements
without personal attacks. Give credit for others' work and protect private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing and
repeated disruption are unacceptable. Do not use project discussions to coordinate
unauthorized access or to distribute confidential material.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for the
project, and to conduct when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Filip Volf at
[filipwolf@gmail.com](mailto:filipwolf@gmail.com), the address published on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Reckless98). Describe what happened,
include relevant links and state any privacy concerns. Do not publish another
person's private details in an issue.

The maintainer will consider context and severity, protect reports as far as
practicable, and may request a correction, remove content, limit participation or
ban an account. Serious threats or repeated misconduct can lead directly to removal.
No response-time or absolute-confidentiality guarantee is made.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the
same private channel and provide additional evidence. This is a single-maintainer
project; there is currently no independent appeals body. Reports concerning the
maintainer may also be made through GitHub's own reporting mechanisms.

This is a project-specific policy, not a claim of formal adoption or certification
under a third-party community standard.

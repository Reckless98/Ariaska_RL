# Research and maintenance roadmap

This roadmap records proposed work, not delivery dates or established capabilities.
The priority is a rights-cleared, reproducible research baseline.

| Priority | Work | Evidence needed for completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Resolve ownership and third-party terms | Maintainer-attested contribution map; per-asset origin/license records; disposition of incompatible corpora and derivatives; reviewed license decision |
| 1 | Prepare a scoped research preview | Reproducible source manifest, reviewed notices, accurate release notes and successful applicable validation at the approved commit |
| 1 | Improve dependency integrity | Locked supported environments, advisory review, reviewed updates and compatibility evidence; do not equate a scan with absence of risk |
| 2 | Evaluate default arbitration | Fixed-budget protocol comparing policy/rule/mentor choices with executed-outcome attribution; held-out data and per-role results |
| 2 | Expand synthetic evaluation | More predeclared seeds and task families; feature-identical inference comparison; preserve negative results and uncertainty |
| 2 | Test mentor value and retention | Authorized, appropriately licensed inputs; mentor-free baseline; explicit model/version, budget and intervention counts; useful transfer measured after mentor removal |
| 2 | Define resume guarantees | A documented boundary for environment/controller state; independent reproduction of supported CPU behavior and separately labeled GPU evidence |
| 3 | Measure auxiliary learners | Isolated ablations for BC, DAgger consumption, reward learning and meta-learning; demonstrate effects rather than infer them from module presence |
| 3 | Establish external reproducibility | Independently reported runs linked to exact source/configuration; count external adoption only when documented |

New work should not expand target-facing functionality as a side effect of
maintenance. No live-provider, GPU or real-world security result is implied by
this roadmap. The [historical phase archive](docs/history/architecture-and-phases.md)
preserves earlier design descriptions without making them current acceptance criteria.
